The Case

Fairbanks is not broken.
Its leadership is.

The people here are builders, problem-solvers, survivors. Nearly 95,000 of them live in a place that demands more resilience before breakfast than most cities ask of people in a year. The infrastructure is aging. The economy stalls. Nothing ambitious has been attempted in decades. This isn't an Alaska problem. It's a leadership vacuum. Vacuums get filled. The question is by whom.

"Fairbanks was founded by people who had no reason to believe it would work. That's exactly why it did."

The Standard

What we're
looking for.

Judgment

The ability to weigh competing priorities and make defensible decisions under pressure. Not ideology. Not dogma. Judgment.

Ethics

No active conflicts. Transparent financial history. Accountable to constituents, not donors. Full stop.

Execution

A track record of finishing things. Projects, commitments, hard conversations. Governance is project management with higher stakes.

Temperament

Steady under attack. Willing to be wrong. Capable of working with people who disagree. The frontier was not built by people who needed everyone to like them.
